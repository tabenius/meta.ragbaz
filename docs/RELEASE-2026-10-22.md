## What changed

Checks show current rendering diagnostics and the active source's content digest,
not a claim about authorship, facts or freshness. Inspection shows inert Markdown
without loading linked sources. Invalid UTF-8 files preserve the current document;
valid BOM and CRLF bytes remain part of its digest.

reports; neutral packages use strict generation. The renderer pin also includes
the earlier manuscript-only print and portable prepared-assets changes.
