# Aether — studies in affection

A newly authored family, not a reconstruction of earlier correspondence.
Its vocabulary is affection expressed as natural philosophy: observations,
correspondences, transformations and incomplete diagrams. These are poetic
studies, not scientific findings, historical facsimiles or symbolic authorities.

**Prima materia** begins before form. Unruled cream, ochre, clay, scattered gold;
an incomplete vessel gathers marks without enclosing them. IM Fell English titles
and Crimson Pro body text are the curated remote alternatives. The composition
should feel like a careful first observation, with more margin than certainty.

**Sun and moon** is correspondence without erasure. Warm gold and cool silver
approach across a quiet axis. Two bodies remain whole; the gap is deliberate.
Cormorant Garamond titles and Spectral body text support a lighter, more balanced
folio. The drawing is original, not an attribution to any historical manuscript.

**Blackening** leaves a place to continue. Plum-black paper, bone letters and an
interrupted copper line do not promise a revelation or explain anyone's loss.
Cormorant Infant titles and EB Garamond body text make the page intimate without
turning it into theatre. Its native palette is dark; an explicit light-paper
appearance and light printing remain available.

The family shares the bounded reader, an optional symbolic plate, offline system
font fallbacks and explicit Google-font alternatives. Variant navigation never
forwards a manuscript URL; changing presentation is not permission to carry
private source identifiers to another page. No personal recovered material is
part of the specimens or the deployable package.
