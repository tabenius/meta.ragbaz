# Heartfulness — first proof

The working name is a feeling, not a permanent brand decision. The visual thread
is the kept letter: something unfolded, reread and left beside a cup of tea.
It is a contemporary, affectionate Victorian/late-romantic British bookish
interpretation, not a historical facsimile or a claim to an unbroken tradition.

Pale butter-yellow paper holds the composition together. Fine, static grain
suggests fibres rather than coffee stains; wear belongs in quiet colour and
soft contrast, not in unreadable text. A meticulously drawn double rule gives
the page a book-cover rhythm, with real breathing room inside it.

Climbing stems are deliberately asymmetric before they are reflected into a
frame. The original rose plates use fine contour lines, small veined leaves and
restrained faded-pink petals. Their rhythm should feel carefully tended rather
than mechanically stamped; the detail is concentrated at the edges, away from
the words. The small divider is a related sprig, not a second competing motif.

Typography pairs a generous italic serif title with a legible bookish text face.
System Baskerville/Palatino/Georgia stacks keep this first proof offline and
free from remote-font permissions. The composition should retain its balance
across those substitutions, rather than promising identical platform metrics.

The baker's method is the design process: mix, knead, bake, taste, repeat.
Plain, A little and Full bloom are tasting portions, not separate documents.
The paper grain is independently switchable. Each refinement should improve
spacing and drawing before introducing another decorative idea.

## Boundaries

- Template source and original ornamental SVGs remain shared host-owned assets.
- Manuscript Markdown stays separate and cannot supply executable SVG or imports.
- The neutral opening and starter are fictional design specimens, not an archive.
- This name does not imply an affiliation with organizations using Heartfulness.
- Local package and route configuration are prepared; no Heartfulness deployment
  or public route activation is recorded by this design proof.
